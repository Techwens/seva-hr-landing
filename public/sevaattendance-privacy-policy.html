SevaAttendance Privacy Policy

Effective: 17 August 2026

SevaAttendance is a managed workplace attendance application provided by Techwens for organisations that use SevaHR. This policy explains how the SevaAttendance Android app accesses, collects, uses, transmits, retains and protects data.

Roles and scope

Your employer or organisation determines why employee attendance and biometric data is processed and is normally the data controller or data fiduciary. Techwens processes that data to provide SevaHR and SevaAttendance under its agreement with the organisation. The app is not a standalone consumer service and employees do not create an account in it.

Data the app processes

  • Employee information: internal employee ID/code, name, department, designation, employment status, workplace assignment and profile-photo reference.
  • Attendance: arrival, departure and break timestamps, device and shift context, match method and confidence, duplicate status and optional local evidence photo.
  • Biometric enrollment and recognition: enrollment photos, mathematical face templates, pose/model metadata, consent timestamp, liveness and presentation-attack signals, and limited face-decision diagnostics. Camera frames are analysed transiently; the app does not record continuous video.
  • Device and security: managed device/installation identifier, tenant/location assignment, pairing epoch, app/version, battery/storage/memory state, queue/sync state and Google Play Integrity verdict.
  • Reliability and analytics: crash reports, performance traces, screen/feature events, pseudonymous attendance/enrollment/sync metrics, and approximate city/country information derived by Firebase Analytics from a masked IP address. Firebase Performance also processes the source IP address to map performance events to a country.

The current app does not request device-location permission or collect precise GPS/device-location sensor coordinates, contacts, microphone, call/SMS logs, calendar, health, financial or advertising-ID data. Attendance records send no device-location coordinates. The limited network-derived approximate geography used for reliability and analytics is described above.

Purposes

Data is used to register authorised employees, verify workplace attendance, work offline, synchronise managed kiosks, prevent spoofing and duplicate punches, investigate recognition and reliability problems, secure the service, support employees and administrators, and meet the organisation’s employment and legal obligations. Techwens does not sell this data or use it for advertising.

Where data goes

The kiosk stores an encrypted offline copy of the roster, face templates, attendance awaiting synchronisation and operational records. Enrollment photos, templates, attendance and diagnostics are transmitted over HTTPS to the organisation’s tenant-scoped SevaHR service. Techwens uses infrastructure/service providers including Google Firebase (analytics, performance and crash reporting), Google Play Integrity (security attestation) and Sentry (error monitoring). Firebase also processes masked/source IP information for coarse geographic analytics and country-level performance reporting. These providers process limited data to provide their services and are not authorised by Techwens to use employee biometric or attendance data for advertising.

Consent and choice

Before face enrollment, the kiosk displays an employee-specific biometric disclosure and requires an affirmative “I Consent” action. Declining does not create a face template. An employee may contact the organisation’s HR administrator to withdraw consent or request another attendance arrangement, subject to the organisation’s policies and applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Local attendance, evidence photos, diagnostics and audit records are removed according to server-configured retention periods. Staged enrollment photos are deleted from the kiosk after successful upload. Server data is retained under the organisation’s retention schedule and applicable legal obligations. Employees may request access, correction, consent withdrawal or deletion through their HR administrator; the organisation verifies identity and scope before an authorised deletion is performed. Some attendance or employment records may be retained where law requires it.

Security

Controls include SQLCipher-encrypted local storage, Android secure secret storage, HTTPS-only production communication, short-lived pairing codes, pairing-bound device tokens, tenant isolation, server-authorised identity-changing operations, presentation-attack controls, Play Integrity verification, role-based access, attributable command/audit records, and release-window protection against screenshots, screen recording, casting and recent-task previews. No system can be guaranteed completely secure; suspected incidents should be reported promptly.

Individual requests and contact

For access, correction, consent or deletion requests, contact your HR administrator first so the request can be linked to the correct organisation and employee. Privacy or security questions for Techwens can be sent to support@techwens.com.
